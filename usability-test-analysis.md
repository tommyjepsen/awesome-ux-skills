# Usability test analysis

Turn sessions into a short list of problems the team will actually fix.

## Steps

1. **Per task:** success, partial or failure for each participant, time on task, and the ease score. Present as a small table.
2. **Log every observation** with participant id, task, what happened, and a quote or timestamp.
3. **Group into issues.** For each issue: how many participants hit it (for example 4 of 6), where in the flow, what they expected, what happened instead.
4. **Severity** from impact and frequency:
   - Critical: blocks the task, or causes a costly mistake
   - Serious: major delay or frustration, many participants
   - Minor: slows or annoys, recovers on their own
5. **Fix** for each issue, specific enough to design from, plus what to re test.
6. **What worked:** things to keep, with evidence.

## Output

The task table, then issues ordered by severity, each with count, evidence, and fix; then what worked; then open questions for the next round.

## Don't

- Don't report a single participant's opinion as a pattern, but do report a single critical failure. Don't mix in your own design preferences as findings. Don't name participants; use ids.
